# v0.7.0-dev.1 — Local motion-foundation validation

Based on released v0.6.9.3. Local development build; final public release remains v0.7.0. No settings/range/density migration.

## What to expect

Motion analytics is enabled for SBS and standard aircraft.json only. Aircraft popups add Observed motion, with supported steady-flight, climb/descent, speed-trend and turn labels. Labels need at least 30 seconds of sufficiently fresh, independent position support. Some aircraft will remain warming up or unclassified. No orbit, holding, destination-divergence, CPA or formation labels are implemented yet.

TFMS and flights.js continue their usual feed/map/density functions and destination ETA, with no motion collection or popup labels. Motion does not alter aircraft.json or count diagnostic requests as aircraft-feed consumers.

## Live checks

1. Back up settings and density/range data, install locally, restart, and confirm /status reports 0.7.0-dev.1. If Dashboard integration is enabled and its companion package was updated, restart Home Assistant Core and refresh the browser too.
2. On SBS or standard aircraft.json, open /motion/status. Confirm available=true, the supported-source list and accepted/sample counters grow. Compare last_batch_seconds and host resource use with your baseline.
3. Let fresh positioned traffic run for 30–60 seconds. Open several popups and compare Observed motion with actual trajectories and reported track/speed/vertical rate. Steady flight is a measured stable-state combination, not a cruise-phase assertion.
4. Inspect /motion?hex=<aircraft-id>&events=1 for support duration, time basis, altitude timing, segment reason, metrics and bounded completed summaries. Exact formulas/thresholds are in motion-primitives-v0.7.0-dev.1.md; no code reading is required.
5. Stop the source briefly. After more than 15 seconds of position age, labels should be stale. A position gap over 30 seconds or an implausible step resets the analytical segment on new data. Feed failure behavior is otherwise unchanged.
6. On TFMS and flights.js, confirm /motion/status reports available=false and retained_samples=0; no Observed motion row appears. Known destinations with usable speed can still show ETA.
7. Verify existing density/range data, exports/restores, dashboard card and aircraft consumers continue to operate. Restart resets motion history only; ordinary persistent data remains.
