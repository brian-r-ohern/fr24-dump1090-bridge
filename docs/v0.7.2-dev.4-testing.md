# v0.7.2-dev.4 — 2026-10-18

Development preview; v0.7.1 remains the stable release. Includes dev.3 startup/defaults repair.

## Changes and user checks

- History controls are **Export selected** and **Export all**. Both download lossless `.json.gz`; the one-hour retention limit is unchanged. Uncompressed API downloads remain available by omitting `compression=gzip`.
- Data management exports density as streaming `.json.gz`. Restore accepts either existing JSON or gzip by file magic, without changing source attribution, validation or merge rules. Decompression stages on disk; allow free space for decompressed JSON plus existing restore staging. No ZIP density import.
- `python tools/replay_aircraft_history.py recording.json.gz -o replay.json` accepts JSON, gzip or a ZIP with exactly one JSON member. Output remains JSON. This is motion replay, not a replay of the browser touchdown model; sampled exports can differ from live classification.
- Popups show airport context separately from observed geometry. Context is retained in sampled motion annotations and current motion snapshots. Missing route metadata can prevent approach/departure context. There is no change to automatic FAA bridging restrictions or trusted-network rules.

## Airport-context rules

Require at least three numeric barometric altitude observations spanning 30 seconds in the latest contiguous segment, within a 120-second window. Require numeric latest altitude and known field elevation. Height is reported barometric altitude minus airport elevation, not measured AGL. Discard positions more than 500 feet below field elevation. Stale motion snapshots suppress context.

| Context | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Likely approach | Known destination; within 60 NM and 18,000 feet above field; altitude endpoint trend at most −300 ft/min; range decreases at least 0.5 NM over support window |
| Likely departure | Known origin; within 40 NM and 18,000 feet above field; trend at least +300 ft/min; range increases at least 0.5 NM |
| Likely airport maneuvering | Nearby airport within 10 NM and 4,000 feet above field; active turn primitive or geometry |
| Possible airport circuit | Same nearby-airport gates plus at least two distinct turn support starts of 135° or greater in latest segment over 15 minutes |

Route-based context takes precedence, then nearest qualifying field. Nearby fields use a spatial grid. Circuit wording is deliberately tentative: this does not prove runway alignment, touch-and-go, clearance, landing or pilot intent. Distinct turn starts can include parts of irregular maneuvering; this is context, not an established pattern. Geometry labels, precedence, scores and thresholds remain unchanged. Airport context does not modify ETT.

## Captured-record replay review

Local observations were analyzed privately; captured aircraft records are not included in this source package.

- EIN107: replay produces 28 sampled constant-radius classifications. Latest phase sequence includes a 315-second right turn (223.5°), a 36-second left correction (−10.8°), then a 217-second right turn (234.2°), followed by 24 seconds nearly straight. The correction divides the continuous-turn detector; adding both turns would violate its uninterrupted-turn assumption. A whole-loop/variable-radius detector needs separate evidence and negative tests, not merely lower orbit thresholds.
- FIN15: replay produces 19 sampled constant-radius classifications. Alternating left/right turns, long straight segments and partial turns do not satisfy a repeated same-direction racetrack grammar. Recorded path is useful arrival-related maneuvering evidence, not confirmed holding intent.
- Full-hour archive: 432 tracks, 49,229 samples, 3,594 seconds. Embedded live annotations contain 100 constant-radius candidate records and 31 reversal candidate records after deduplication by name/support start; these are label episodes, not independent maneuvers. No recorded orbit or racetrack. This scan is not independent proof of absence.
- Negative evaluation cases: broad BOS departure turns (JBU551, JBU2185, DAL508, DAL571, UAL3772); airport-arrival reversals (AAL1438, ENY3531, UAL1977); Quonset circuits (N4900H); irregular Cessna turns (N7543G). Use private replay inputs with the CLI. Do not silently turn their inferred intent into ground-truth training labels.
