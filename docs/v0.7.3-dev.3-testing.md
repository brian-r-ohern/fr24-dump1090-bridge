# v0.7.3-dev.3 testing

Hit **Ctrl+Shift+R** to refresh before installing an update.

## Recording controls

The app configuration option **Show development recording controls** (`development_recording`) defaults to off. Enable, save, restart, and refresh to show Development recording in Map controls. Visibility does not stop or erase a recording already started; stop it before hiding the panel when desired.

## Local route history

Live RAM history remains a rolling hour. Additional history is stored in source-specific SQLite files under `/data/local-history`, sampled at the existing 10-second history cadence, and retained for at most 24 hours, including after restart. Selected history and Export all merge these records with the rolling hour. Motion classification still uses its existing bounded windows.

Qualification requires both reported origin and destination to resolve to airport coordinates inside local coverage, plus a retained observation within 10 NM of either airport. This is an observed proximity rule, not proof of departure or landing. FAA coverage uses the configured geographic gate; other sources use the airport bearing's receiver range bin, or stored density cells in the airport neighborhood (approximately ±0.08° latitude and ±0.1° longitude). Density is independent of the current map viewport. No home position means coverage is not established.

The first qualification backfills the available rolling hour, then appends accepted samples. It cannot recover earlier points already expired. Missing or unresolvable route endpoints, and distant-origin flights such as LAX–BOS, retain the rolling hour. Changes to flight or reported route reset that track's stored route history. Sources have separate stores. Inactive qualifying tracks remain exportable until their samples expire after 24 hours. The experiment recorder remains a separate opt-in product.

## Destination overlay

Open an aircraft popup. When its reported destination resolves, an amber × marks the airport and an amber dashed line joins it to the aircraft. Updates follow the selected aircraft; closing the popup, switching selection, or losing the track removes the old overlay. It depicts a direct connection to reported destination, not a predicted route. No destination/coordinates means no overlay.
