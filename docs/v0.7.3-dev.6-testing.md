## v0.7.3-dev.6 — projected passes near Home

Hit **Ctrl+Shift+R** before updating.

Aircraft whose current observed three-dimensional velocity projects them within **3 NM slant range of Home during the next five minutes** receive a cyan ring. Toggle it under Motion. The aircraft popup shows predicted slant distance, time, horizontal distance, vertical difference, and altitude basis. This uses Home's configured elevation and fresh, contiguous position/altitude support. Missing elevation, altitude or velocity suppresses the highlight. Maneuvering predictions are marked lower confidence. Barometric altitude relative to Home elevation is approximate; this is a display aid, not a collision warning.

Includes the grouped-configuration startup fix and previous history/destination features. The build is a release-validation candidate for v0.7.3.
