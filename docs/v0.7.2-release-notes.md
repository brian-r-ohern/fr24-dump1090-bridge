# FR24 dump1090 Bridge v0.7.2

Version 0.7.2 expands aircraft motion analysis with repeated geometric patterns,
local track history, and nearby-aircraft comparisons.

- **Motion geometry:** constant-radius turns, orbit candidates, repeated orbits,
  course reversals, and racetrack/holding-like patterns with supporting evidence.
- **Track history:** rolling one-hour trails and compressed GeoJSON exports for
  selected or all aircraft, tested in QGIS. Selected filenames include the
  aircraft's reported ICAO identifier, with a local-ID fallback.
- **Proximity analysis:** up to ten aircraft within 10 NM horizontally, ordered
  by approximate slant range, with separation, horizontal closing speed, and
  five-minute constant-velocity CPA/TCPA estimates where supported.
- **Destination movement:** Moving toward and Moving away observations, with
  sustained movement-away suppression during established holding-like geometry.
- **Usability and reliability:** persistent expanded popup details, collapsible
  map controls, source-switch handling, improved SBS recovery, and experimental
  touchdown scenarios that respond to observed descent.

Motion classifications and predictions remain experimental, with evidence and
quality information available for inspection. They describe observed behavior
without establishing intent or assessing safe separation. FAA sources retain
local analytical functionality while automatic aircraft-feed bridging remains disabled.

**Before installing the update, press Ctrl+Shift+R to refresh the page.**

History is transient and clears on restart. Destination metadata age is unknown.
Rare pattern detections remain open to further field evaluation; go-around,
figure-eight, formation and composite maneuver classifiers are future work.

Validation: user confirmed dev.9 checks and QGIS display. The final 10 NM radius
and display wording were checked in source/tests, rather than installed as dev.9.
The source archive has not been container-built or installed on HAOS here.
